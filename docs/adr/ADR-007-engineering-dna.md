# ADR-007 — Engineering DNA

- Status: Accepted
- Context: Geometry recognition alone cannot infer engineering meaning.
- Problem: Feature/function/manufacturing knowledge was implicit in algorithms.
- Alternatives: hard-coded heuristics; external knowledge service; local versioned ontology/rules.
- Decision: Store structured, versioned and extensible engineering knowledge with provenance, rules, patterns and probabilities.
- Consequences: conclusions become explainable; normative values remain external/verified inputs.
- Future impact: datasets and licensed standards require schema governance and intellectual-property controls.
