# ADR-014: Layer professional recognition over URF

Status: Accepted

## Decision

UERS composes specialist recognizers as URF plugins and performs multi-pass topology, feature, pattern and engineering inference above immutable primitive results. EDE receives the final report; specialist modules do not silently execute reconstruction.

## Consequences

Mathematical fitting remains reusable across Mobile, Desktop and Cloud. Engineering interpretation stays probabilistic and traceable. Unsupported feature boundaries, symmetry modes and manufacturing evidence remain explicit extension points rather than simulated capability.
