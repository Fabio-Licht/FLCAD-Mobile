# R2-006A — Visual Experiment History

## Experiment 1 — Solid Silhouette

Status: **COMPLETED — REJECTED BY OPERATIONAL TEST**

The Native GPU geometry was presented as one uniform solid color, without
lighting, specular response, shadows, ambient occlusion, material response or
other photometric information.

The operator continued to perceive the small rotation during Pan. Removing the
photometric information also destroyed the viewport's visual quality.

### Conclusion

The rotation perceived during Pan is not caused primarily by lighting or
material. This hypothesis is closed.

The experimental presentation was removed and the previously approved
technical material and lighting were restored in full.
