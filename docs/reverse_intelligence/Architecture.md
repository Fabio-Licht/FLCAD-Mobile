# AREI Architecture

AREI is an evidence-driven reasoning layer above Engineering Core, Geometric Kernel and Smart Regions. Its dependency flow is observation → classification → hypotheses → planning → competing strategies → validation → decision → explanation → learning. The resulting `ReasoningSnapshot` is the reverse-engineering digital twin.

The brain does not generate final CAD, B-Rep, booleans or reconstructed surfaces. ML, GPU, Cloud and specialized agents connect through contracts and may enrich measured evidence without replacing provenance.
