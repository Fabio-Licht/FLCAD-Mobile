# Surface Quality Analytics

Analytics records processing duration, patch count, G0/G1/G2/not-applicable distribution, average curvature, reflection score, draft score and overall quality. Reports persist Project First under `CAD/Continuity`, `CAD/SurfaceQuality`, `CAD/Curvature`, `CAD/Zebra`, `CAD/Reflection`, `CAD/Draft`, `CAD/QualityReports` and `CAD/QualityHistory`.
