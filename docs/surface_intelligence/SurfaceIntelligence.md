# Surface Intelligence

`core/surface_intelligence` converts engineering evidence into an explainable surface reconstruction plan. It consumes Recognition, Professional Recognition, AREI, Engineering DNA, Cognition, Decision Engine and Reconstruction Planner conclusions through portable evidence adapters.

The domain never calls Geometry Kernel, CAD Builder or Feature Engine creation APIs. Those modules are downstream consumers of an accepted future plan only. G-005A produces no surface, B-Rep or `ShapeHandle`.
